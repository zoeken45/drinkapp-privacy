Kısaca

DrinkApp'in bir hesabı, sunucusu, reklamı ve analitik aracı yoktur. Kaydettiğiniz içecek verileri **yalnızca
telefonunuzda** saklanır ve bize ulaşmaz.

Hangi veriler saklanır ve nerede?

Aşağıdaki veriler uygulamanın cihazınızdaki veritabanında tutulur:

  • İçecek kayıtlarınız: içecek türü, miktar, tarih ve saat, isteğe bağlı not.
  • Profiliniz: kilo, cinsiyet, isteğe bağlı boy, ölçü birimi, su hedefi, kafein ve alkol limitleri.
  • Ayarlarınız: bildirim tercihleri, sessiz saatler, Sağlık uygulaması tercihi ve kendi eklediğiniz özel içecekler.
  • Yaş beyanınız: ilk açılışta "18 yaşında veya daha büyüğüm" dediğinizi gösteren tek bir evet işareti. Doğum tarihiniz veya kimlik bilginiz istenmez ve saklanmaz.

Cinsiyet ve kilo yalnızca alkol tahmini ve su hedefi gibi hesaplamalar için kullanılır. Bu veriler başka bir yere gönderilmez. Alkol tahmini (Pro), su önerisi ve kafein uyku ipucu da tamamen cihazınızda hesaplanır.

Sağlık uygulamasına yazma (isteğe bağlı)

Siz açarsanız DrinkApp, kaydettiğiniz su ve kafein miktarlarını iOS'ta Apple Sağlık'a (HealthKit), Android'de
Health Connect'e yazar. Alkol verisi yazılmaz. DrinkApp Sağlık uygulamanızdan hiçbir veri okumaz. İzni iOS Ayarlar
veya Health Connect üzerinden istediğiniz zaman kapatabilirsiniz. Daha önce yazılan kayıtlar Sağlık uygulamanızda kalır
ve oradan silinebilir. Sağlık verileriniz reklam veya pazarlama amacıyla kullanılmaz ve üçüncü taraflarla paylaşılmaz.

In short

DrinkApp has no account, server, ads or analytics. The drink data you log is stored only on your phone and never
reaches us.

What is stored, and where?

The following is kept in the app's on-device database:

  • Your drink log: drink type, amount, date and time, an optional note.
  • Your profile: weight, sex, optional height, unit, water goal, caffeine and alcohol limits.
  • Your settings: notification preferences, quiet hours, health-app preference and any custom drinks you create.
  • Your age declaration: a single yes marker showing that you said "I am 18 or older" on first launch. Your birth date or ID is never asked for or stored.

Weight and sex are used only for calculations such as the alcohol estimate and the water goal. They are not sent anywhere. The alcohol estimate (Pro), the water suggestion and the caffeine sleep hint are also calculated entirely on your device.

Writing to your health app (optional)

If you turn it on, DrinkApp writes the water and caffeine you log to Apple Health (HealthKit) on iOS and to
Health Connect on Android. Alcohol is never written. DrinkApp does not read any data from your health app. You can
revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings or Health Connect. Records already written stay in your health app and
can be deleted there. Your health data is not used for advertising or marketing and is not shared with third parties.

Notifications

Reminders and alerts are created locally on your device. No server is used to send them. You can turn the permission off
in system settings at any time.

Purchases (Pro subscription)

Subscription payments are processed by Apple (the App Store); we never see your card details. To verify that your
subscription is active we use RevenueCat as the subscription infrastructure. RevenueCat processes a randomly
generated anonymous user ID, purchase/subscription information and technical device information. This does not include
your drink log or health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy
This only happens when you use the purchase screen or your subscription status is checked.

Exporting your data

In Settings you can export your entries as a CSV file. The file is created temporarily on your device, sent to the app or
place you choose, and deleted once sharing finishes. DrinkApp does not upload it to any server; the privacy rules of
wherever you send it (email, cloud storage, etc.) are that service's own.

Deleting your data

Your data lives only on your device; deleting the app deletes it. You can also delete individual entries inside the app.
Records written to your health app can be deleted from Health or Health Connect. You manage or cancel your subscription
in your Apple ID settings.

Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data on a server, access, correction and deletion are mostly handled directly in
the app. For any question, contact support.drinkapp@gmail.com. Your rights under applicable law (such as GDPR and Turkey's KVKK)
are unaffected.

Important notice

Alcohol values (for example the estimated blood alcohol level) are estimates based on standard formulas. They are not
a medical or legal measurement and must not be used to decide whether you can drive. The app does not give medical advice.

Children

DrinkApp includes alcohol tracking and is not intended for users under 18. On first launch the app asks you to declare that you are 18 or older; this is a self-declaration, and age is not verified. If you say you are under 18, the app stays blocked. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and the "Last updated" date will change.
